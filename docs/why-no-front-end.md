# Why this runs without a front end

An operations owner asks who is waiting, what is overdue and why a decision was made. The database and approval commands answer those questions. Read-only HTML gives the weekly meeting and decision paperwork a printable view. brand.json sets the business name, logo and colours.

A screen also gives form validation as you type, mobile entry, offline capture, drag and drop and identity-based access. This base does not provide those. It does not send alerts or run background schedules. An operator runs each command. Local PGlite supports one process at a time. Shared Postgres is a deployment option once authentication, permissions and backups have been designed and tested. Enterprise DNA can build the required web front end, integrations or different stack into the business's version through Omni by Enterprise DNA.
